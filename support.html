Hooper Support

How to drive

Open Garage, choose a vehicle and terrain, then tap Drive. Move the lower-right thumb control up to accelerate, down to brake or reverse, and left or right to steer. Use Rescue after a rollover.

Vehicle purchases

Hooper and Apex Frame are included free. Other vehicles have a one-second driving preview and can be permanently unlocked individually. Use Restore Purchases in the purchase screen to recover previous purchases made with the same Apple Account.

Need more help?

Report a problem through the public Hooper support tracker. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description. Do not post purchase receipts or other private information.
