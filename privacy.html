Hooper Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026

Data collection

Hooper does not require an account and does not collect analytics, advertising data, precise location, contacts, photos, health data or tracking identifiers. Driving simulation state stays on the device.

Purchases

Vehicle purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Hooper receives verified purchase entitlement information needed to unlock a vehicle. The developer does not receive or store your payment-card details. Apple processes purchase information under Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

Hooper does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game contains no chat, user accounts or targeted advertising.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated if Hooper’s data practices change. Privacy questions can be submitted through the public Hooper support tracker. Do not post private information there.
